Site Challenges

Construction Workforce Problems
That Cost Projects Dearly

With hundreds of workers across multiple sites and a mix of permanent and contract labour, construction is among the hardest industries to manage HR for.

Paper-Based Muster Rolls

Manual muster rolls at construction sites are prone to ghost entries, proxy attendance, and data loss — leading to inflated labour bills.

Untracked Sub-Contractor Labour

Managing dozens of sub-contractors with separate pay cycles, statutory deductions, and compliance obligations creates costly blind spots.

Labour Act Non-Compliance Risk

Construction sites are frequently audited under the Contract Labour Act, BOCWA, and ESIC. Manual records rarely hold up to inspector scrutiny.
