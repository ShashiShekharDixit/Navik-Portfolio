Why Healthcare Workforce Management
Demands a Different Approach

Hospitals and clinics operate 24/7 with a complex mix of clinical and non-clinical staff across departments — making HR management genuinely critical to patient care quality.

24/7 Critical Shift Gaps

An absent nurse or doctor with no real-time alert system can leave entire wards understaffed — a direct risk to patient outcomes and hospital reputation.

Complex Multi-Department Rosters

Scheduling across ICU, OPD, Emergency, Radiology, and support departments — each with different skill requirements — is impossible to do manually at scale.

Credential & Licence Compliance

Tracking medical registrations, nursing council licences, and certifications for hundreds of staff manually is a compliance and legal liability risk.

navik for Healthcare Goes Live
in 4 Steps

Our healthcare-focused onboarding team works with your department heads to configure navik for your hospital's exact structure.

1

Map Departments & Units

Register every department, ward, OT, and support unit. Assign department heads, configure staff roles, and set minimum staffing thresholds per shift.

2

Enrol Staff & Credentials

Upload staff records including medical registrations, nursing licences, and specialisations. navik tracks expiry dates and alerts you automatically before they lapse.

3

Build Shift Rosters

Create weekly rosters for each department, ensuring skill coverage requirements are met for every shift. On-call and emergency duty protocols are built right in.

4

Automate Payroll & Compliance

Run monthly payroll for all staff grades — consultants, full-time, part-time, contract — with automatic PF, ESI, and TDS calculations and digital payslip distribution.
