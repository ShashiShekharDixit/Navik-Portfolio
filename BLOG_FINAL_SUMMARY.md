## 🎨 Design System Applied

### Colors
- Primary Blue: #2563eb
- Dark Navy: #0f1f4b
- Light Background: #f0f9ff
- Gradients: 135deg blue combinations
- Shadows: Layered depth effects

### Typography
- Headings: 800 weight, -0.05em letter-spacing
- Buttons: 600-700 weight
- Body: 400-500 weight
- Sizes: Responsive with clamp()

### Spacing
- Search gap: 12px
- Category gap: 14px
- Card grid gap: 32px
- Card padding: 28px
- Pagination gap: 20px

### Animations
- Timing: 0.3-0.4 seconds
- Easing: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)
- Effects: Bouncy, delightful
- Performance: GPU-accelerated
