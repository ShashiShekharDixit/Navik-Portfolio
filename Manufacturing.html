Plant Challenges

Manufacturing HR Runs on
Razor-Thin Margins for Error

Rotating shifts, contract workers, multiple production lines, and strict factory act compliance make manufacturing one of the most demanding HR environments.

Complex Rotating Shift Systems

A, B, and C shifts, night shift premiums, weekly-off rotations, and cross-department swaps create scheduling nightmares with manual systems.

High Contract Labour Volume

Plants often have 60–70% contract workers managed by multiple contractors, each with different wage rates, PF applicability, and compliance requirements.

Factory Act & ESI Compliance

Maintaining Form 12, Form 25, overtime registers, and ESI/PF challans manually leads to compliance gaps during factory inspector visits.
