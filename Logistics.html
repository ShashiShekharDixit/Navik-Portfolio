Logistics Teams Face Unique
Workforce Complexities

Drivers, dispatchers, loaders, and field agents — managing them all with spreadsheets leads to errors, delays, and compliance failures.

Scattered Shift Schedules

Drivers and warehouse staff on rotating 24/7 shifts create scheduling nightmares with last-minute changes going untracked.

Manual Overtime Calculations

Calculating overtime, per-diem, and route bonuses manually for hundreds of drivers wastes HR hours and causes payroll errors.

No Real-time Field Visibility

Without GPS-linked attendance, knowing whether field staff are at the right location at the right time is nearly impossible.

Built for Every Role in Logistics

navik is engineered for the complexity of logistics — from driver check-ins to warehouse payroll runs.

GPS-Based Driver Attendance

Drivers punch in from their location. Geofencing auto-validates check-ins against delivery points and depot locations.

Dynamic Shift Scheduling

Build and modify shifts for morning, evening, and night runs. Auto-notify drivers of schedule changes via SMS and app.

Route-Based Payroll Automation

Automatically calculate per-trip allowances, overtime, and fuel reimbursements based on actual route data logged by the driver.

Contract Labour Management

Manage daily-wage loaders, contract drivers, and third-party staff alongside permanent employees — all under one dashboard.

Compliance & Licence Tracking

Track driver licence expiry, vehicle fitness certificates, and labour compliance deadlines — get alerts before they lapse.

Operations Analytics

Real-time dashboards showing fleet utilisation, absenteeism rates, overtime spend, and payroll variance across all depots.
