Salary Calculation - Indian Labor Compliance

Gross Salary = Basic + HRA + DA + Other Allowances
Net Salary (Take Home) = Gross Salary - Deductions
CTC (Cost to Company) = Gross Salary + Employer Contributions (EPF 8.33%, ESI 3.25% if applicable)

Important Notes:
• EPF calculated on ceiling of ₹15,000/month (Jan 2024 norms)
• ESI applicable only if gross salary ≤ ₹21,000
• Deductions should include PF, income tax, professional tax, etc.
