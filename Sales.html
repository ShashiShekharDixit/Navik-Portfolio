Why Managing Sales Teams
Is Harder Than It Looks

Sales teams are dispersed, target-driven, and variable in their pay structures — making HR management uniquely complex without the right tools.

No Field Visibility

Without GPS tracking, sales managers have no way to verify if field reps are at client locations or following planned beat routes during working hours.

Incentive Calculation Disputes

Complex slab-based incentives, contest bonuses, and clawbacks calculated manually in Excel lead to disputes, demotivation, and high attrition.

Delayed Performance Insights

By the time monthly reports are compiled, the window to coach underperformers and reward top performers has already passed.
