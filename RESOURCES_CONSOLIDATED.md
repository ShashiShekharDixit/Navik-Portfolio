## 🔗 Backend Integration

### Blog Links (from backend)
Replace `#` with your blog URLs:

```
Latest Articles     → /blog or /blog/latest
HR Tips & Tricks    → /blog/category/tips
Industry Updates    → /blog/category/industry
Product Updates     → /blog/category/product
```

### Templates Links (from backend)
Replace `#` with your template URLs:

```
HR Templates        → /templates/hr
Policy Templates    → /templates/policies
Form Templates      → /templates/forms
Report Templates    → /templates/reports
```
