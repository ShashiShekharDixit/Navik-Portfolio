FAQ

Contract workforce management, simplified.

What is Contract Workforce Management?

Contract Workforce Management is the process of managing temporary, outsourced and contract workers throughout their lifecycle, including onboarding, documentation, contracts, deployment, compliance, renewals and offboarding.

Can Navik manage multiple staffing vendors?

The platform can be structured around centralized worker, contract and documentation records, allowing organizations to maintain better visibility across workforce sources.

Can contracts and documents be tracked?

Yes. Contract records, worker documents, expiry dates and approval workflows can be centralized so teams can monitor important actions from one operational view.

Can it connect with HR and payroll processes?

Contract workforce information can be integrated into broader HR, attendance, workforce management and payroll processes depending on the organization's implementation.

Is the platform suitable for large workforces?

The product is designed around centralized workforce data, automated workflows, reporting and operational visibility, making the model suitable for organizations managing distributed or high-volume contract workforces.
