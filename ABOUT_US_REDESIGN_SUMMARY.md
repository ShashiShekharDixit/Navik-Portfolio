# About Us Page - Redesign Complete ✅

## Overview
Successfully redesigned the About Us page with **professional graphics, animations, and modern design elements** - moving away from generic AI-generated content.

## Key Features Implemented

### 1. **Compress-Release Animation for Team Cards**
- Team member cards animate with a spring-like compress-release effect
- Cards scale from 0.6 → 0.95 → 1.0 with staggered delays
- Creates dynamic, engaging entrance animation
- Cubic-bezier timing for smooth spring motion

### 2. **Decorative Graphic Elements**
- **Animated circular orbs** in the background (3 large circles with float animations)
- **Gradient circles** with radial patterns floating independently
- **Team image background orbs** - floating circles within team card images
- Corner decorations and geometric shapes for visual interest

### 3. **Team Showcase Section Enhancements**
- Large animated circular orbs floating in background
- Cards with hover effects:
  - Top gradient bar that appears on hover
  - Gradient line decoration under team info
  - Scale and lift animation on hover
- Floating animation on team member emoji backgrounds

### 4. **Team Moments Showcase Section** (NEW!)
- 6 culture/values cards with **custom SVG graphics**:
  - **Innovation First**: Smiley face circle design with gradient
  - **Growth Together**: Bar chart with gradient bars
  - **Collaboration Hub**: Triangle with connected circles
  - **Customer Love**: 5-point star design
  - **Diversity & Inclusion**: Network of connected circles
  - **Work-Life Balance**: Heart/compass hybrid design

### 5. **Graphic Animations**
- Rotating SVG icons that speed up on hover (20s → 8s rotation)
- Gradient fills with multiple color transitions
- Filter effects (drop-shadow) on graphics
- Smooth scale and position transforms

### 6. **Background Graphics**
- Multiple decorative circles with different opacities
- Floating animations at different speeds (6s, 8s, 10s)
- Radial gradient fills for depth
- Motion blur effect simulation
