Retail Pain Points

The Real Challenges Retail
HR Managers Face Daily

High staff turnover, multi-location scheduling, and seasonal surge hiring make retail one of the hardest industries to manage manually.

High Staff Turnover

Retail sees 40–60% annual turnover. Frequent onboarding, offboarding, and document re-collection drains HR bandwidth every month.

Peak Season Scheduling Chaos

Festive sales, weekends, and promotions demand rapid shift changes. Managing it manually leads to understaffed counters and revenue loss.

Multi-Outlet HR Fragmentation

Each store runs its own attendance registers and payroll sheets. Consolidating data from 20+ outlets for payroll is a monthly nightmare.
